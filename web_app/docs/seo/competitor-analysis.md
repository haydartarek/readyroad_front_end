## Official result competitors

Government pages from Flanders, Wallonia, Brussels, and FPS Mobility rank for exam procedure and legal clarification. ReadyRoad should support rather than displace that intent:

- Keep official-procedure claims out of generic marketing copy.
- Avoid “official questions”, “identical exam”, or guaranteed-success wording.
- Keep region-sensitive facts in governed content and direct users to current official sources.

## Format and UX observations

- Commercial leaders place a test CTA immediately above the fold.
- Topic hubs link to priority, speed, signs, parking, and serious offences.
- FAQ sections capture long-tail questions but often repeat promotional claims.
- Progress tracking and error analysis are common product differentiators.
- Arabic organic web coverage is notably thinner than NL, FR, and EN in the sampled results.

Structured-data implementations were not treated as verified unless visible in the fetched result. No competitor schema claim is used as an implementation requirement.
